# Ahana and the Trains

[NARRATOR] Tonight Ahana was thinking about trains.
[SFX: soft evening breeze, 3s]
[NARRATOR] A soft sound came from nearby, and Mira the Mongoose peeked in.
[MIRA] Hello, Ahana. Shall we go and look, just a little?
[NARRATOR] Ahana nodded, and together they went, slow and easy.
[MIRA] Look here. Isn't trains lovely and quiet tonight?
[SFX: gentle chimes, 2s]
[NARRATOR] There was a tiny puzzle to solve, but it was a friendly one.
[MIRA] We can do it together. Kind and slow does it.
[NARRATOR] Ahana helped, patient and gentle, and the puzzle came right.
[MIRA] You did that so kindly, Ahana. Well done.
[NARRATOR] Now the sky was soft and dark, and it was time to rest.
[MIRA] Goodnight, Ahana. I will be here tomorrow.
[SFX: soft lullaby hum, 4s]
[NARRATOR] Ahana snuggled down, warm and safe, and closed both eyes. Goodnight.
